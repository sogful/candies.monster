as seen by cuttherope.net, a lot of the om nom merch was targeted for russia and not everything escaped it. a good portion of these companies also don't exist anymore, or they just quietly dropped partnerships.
you should be careful with what you purchase some images are sourced from pinterest uploads, specifically the official cut the rope account there or ebay listings. none of the links below are affiliates.
